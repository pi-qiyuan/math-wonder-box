Polar Harmonics

Rose Curve & Maurer Rose

What is a Rose Curve?

A rose curve (also known as a rhodonea curve) is a sinusoid plotted in polar coordinates. These curves are defined by simple polar equations:

r = a cos(kθ)
or
r = a sin(kθ)

Petals and Rationality

The parameter k determines the shape and number of petals. If k is an integer, the curve has k petals if k is odd, and 2k petals if k is even. If k is a rational number, the curve is closed and creates beautifully interlaced, overlapping petals.

Maurer Roses

A Maurer Rose (introduced by Peter M. Maurer in 1987) consists of a line-drawn lattice connecting points of a rose curve. By stepping through the curve with a constant degree step d (e.g., 71° or 97°) and connecting the points in order, an intricate star-like web of 360 lines is formed, revealing hidden geometric harmonies.
