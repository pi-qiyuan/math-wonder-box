Nature's Proportion

Golden Spiral

What is the Golden Spiral?

The golden spiral is a logarithmic spiral whose growth factor is φ, the golden ratio. It gets wider (or further from its origin) by a factor of φ for every quarter turn it makes.

φ = (1 + √5) / 2 ≈ 1.618

The Fibonacci Connection

The golden spiral can be approximated by drawing quarter-circles inside a tiling of squares whose side lengths are Fibonacci numbers: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, and so on.

Beauty in Nature

The golden spiral and the Fibonacci sequence are frequently found in nature, from the arrangement of seeds in a sunflower to the spiral arms of galaxies and the shape of nautilus shells. This proportion is often perceived as visually harmonious and balanced.
