Noise Flow Field

Generative Art through Perlin Noise

The Concept

A Flow Field is a grid of vectors that define a direction and magnitude at every point in space. By placing particles in this field and allowing them to follow the vectors, we create organic, fluid-like patterns.

The Math

The core of this visualization is Perlin Noise, a type of gradient noise developed by Ken Perlin. Unlike purely random values, Perlin noise produces a smooth, continuous sequence of pseudo-random values.

v(x, y) = (cos(α), sin(α))
α = Noise(x, y) * 4π

The velocity v at any point (x, y) is determined by mapping the noise value at that coordinate to an angle α.
