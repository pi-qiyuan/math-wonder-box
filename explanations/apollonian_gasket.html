Descartes' Circle Theorem

Apollonian Gasket

What is an Apollonian Gasket?

An Apollonian gasket is a fractal generated from three mutually tangent circles. By repeatedly filling the gaps between tangent circles with new circles that are tangent to three of the existing ones, an infinite, recursive pattern is formed.

(k₁ + k₂ + k₃ + k₄)² = 2(k₁² + k₂² + k₃² + k₄²)

Descartes' Circle Theorem

The calculation is based on Descartes' Circle Theorem, which relates the curvatures (reciprocals of radii) of four mutually tangent circles. If we know the curvatures of three tangent circles, we can solve for the fourth.

Historical Significance

Named after the Greek mathematician Apollonius of Perga, this problem dates back to antiquity but was given a modern treatment by René Descartes in 1643 and later generalized to Frederick Soddy's famous poem "The Kiss Precise".
